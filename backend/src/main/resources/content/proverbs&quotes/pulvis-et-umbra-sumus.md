# Pulvis et Umbra Sumus

## English translation

We are dust and shadow.

## Author and attribution

Horace

## Meaning

Human life is fragile and temporary. Dust evokes the body’s dissolution; shadow suggests the insubstantial existence of the dead.

## Cultural context

Odes 4.7 contrasts nature’s recurring seasons with the finality of human death. Even the great figures of myth cannot escape that limit.

## Significance and legacy

The phrase offers a memorable statement of mortality and a counterweight to pride. It belongs to the same broad poetic reflection on limited time that informs carpe diem.

## A note on the wording

This is an ancient poetic reflection, not originally a Christian funeral formula, although later readers may hear related religious imagery.
