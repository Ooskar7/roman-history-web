# Docendo discimus

## English translation

By teaching, we learn.

## Author and attribution

Proverbial formulation; related to Seneca

## Meaning

Explaining an idea to someone else forces us to organize it, expose gaps, and deepen our understanding. Teaching and learning can therefore reinforce one another.

## Cultural context

In Letter 7, Seneca advises sharing knowledge with people who can benefit from it and describes learning and teaching as reciprocal activities.

## Significance and legacy

The motto expresses an enduring educational principle: knowledge develops through exchange. It fits tutoring, discussion, and collaborative study as well as formal teaching.

## A note on the wording

Seneca’s text says “homines dum docent discunt” — people learn while they teach. Docendo discimus is a compact related formulation, not his exact wording there.
