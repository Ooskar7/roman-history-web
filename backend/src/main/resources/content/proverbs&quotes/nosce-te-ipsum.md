# Nosce te ipsum

## English translation

Know yourself.

## Author and attribution

Latin rendering of a Greek Delphic maxim; authorship uncertain

## Meaning

Examine your character, abilities, and limits. Self-knowledge includes recognizing what you do not know and resisting exaggerated confidence.

## Cultural context

The Greek maxim is associated with Apollo’s sanctuary at Delphi and the traditions of the Seven Sages. Its Latin reception reflects Rome’s deep engagement with Greek philosophy.

## Significance and legacy

It became a durable starting point for ethical reflection and education. Later readers have applied it to inner character, personal identity, and the limits of human knowledge.

## A note on the wording

It is not originally a Roman invention, and its traditional associations do not establish a single author. Socrates is central to its philosophical reception, not a securely established originator.
