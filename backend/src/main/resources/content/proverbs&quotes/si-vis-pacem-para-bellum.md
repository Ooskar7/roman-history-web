# Si Vis Pacem, Para Bellvm

## English translation

If you want peace, prepare for war.

## Author and attribution

Later formulation of an idea in Vegetius

## Meaning

Readiness for conflict is presented as a way to discourage attack. This is an argument for preparedness, not proof that military expansion always produces peace.

## Cultural context

Vegetius, writing in late antiquity, stresses training, discipline, and preparation in his military handbook. The preface to Book 3 connects a desire for peace with preparing for war.

## Significance and legacy

The maxim has become a durable expression of deterrence thinking. It invites debate about when preparation prevents conflict and when it can worsen rivalry.

## A note on the wording

Bellvm uses V for the vowel now usually printed U: bellum. The familiar slogan condenses Vegetius’s wording, “qui desiderat pacem, praeparet bellum.”
