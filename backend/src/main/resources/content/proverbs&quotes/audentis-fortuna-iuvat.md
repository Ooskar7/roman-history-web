# Audentis Fortuna Iuvat

## English translation

Fortune favors the bold.

## Author and attribution

Virgil; spoken by Turnus in the Aeneid

## Meaning

Opportunity rewards those willing to act despite danger. It encourages courage, but does not promise that every daring choice will succeed.

## Cultural context

In Aeneid 10.284, Turnus urges his men into battle against the newly arrived forces of Aeneas. The epic links Rome’s legendary beginnings to the conflicts and sacrifices of its heroes.

## Significance and legacy

The saying survives as a motto of initiative and bravery. Its dramatic setting adds an irony often lost in modern use: boldness alone cannot rescue Turnus from his eventual defeat.

## A note on the wording

Audentis is a poetic accusative plural form, also written audentes. Fortuna can mean fortune or evoke the personified power of luck.
