# Errare humanum est

## English translation

To err is human.

## Author and attribution

Proverb; no secure single author for this exact form

## Meaning

Making mistakes is part of being human. Recognizing that fallibility can encourage humility and correction rather than denial.

## Cultural context

Cicero expresses a related idea in Philippic 12.5: anyone can make a mistake, but persisting in one is foolish. Similar thoughts recur in later Latin moral writing.

## Significance and legacy

The expression remains a familiar appeal for understanding after a mistake. Read alongside the ancient parallel, it also demands responsibility for learning from error.

## A note on the wording

Often attributed to Seneca without a secure citation. The shorter proverb should be distinguished from Cicero’s longer sentence.
