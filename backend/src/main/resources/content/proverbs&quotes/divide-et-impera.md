# Divide et Impera

## English translation

Divide and rule.

## Author and attribution

Unknown; not securely attested as a classical quotation

## Meaning

A ruler strengthens control by keeping opponents or subject groups divided, making coordinated resistance harder.

## Cultural context

The phrase is often used retrospectively to describe Roman power and relations with subject communities. That interpretive use does not establish that a Roman leader coined the slogan.

## Significance and legacy

It has become a widely used description of political manipulation and imperial control. Applying it to a particular event requires evidence of deliberate division, not just the existence of rival groups.

## A note on the wording

Attributions to Caesar or Philip II should be treated cautiously. The Instituto Cervantes proverb collection notes that the wording is unattested in classical antiquity and likely later.
