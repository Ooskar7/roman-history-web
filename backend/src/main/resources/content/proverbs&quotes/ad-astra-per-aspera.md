# Ad Astra Per Aspera

## English translation

To the stars through hardships.

## Author and attribution

No securely identified author of this exact motto

## Meaning

Great aspirations require endurance through difficulty. The stars stand for a lofty goal, while aspera suggests the rough obstacles on the way.

## Cultural context

The familiar motto has classical parallels rather than a secure origin as this exact Roman quotation. Seneca’s Hercules Furens describes the road from earth to the stars as difficult.

## Significance and legacy

It survives as a motto of perseverance and aspiration. Its appeal rests on joining an ambitious destination with an honest acknowledgment of effort.

## A note on the wording

Also commonly written per aspera ad astra. Do not attribute the exact wording to Seneca merely because his tragedy contains a related thought.
