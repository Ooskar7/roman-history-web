# Veni, Vidi, Vici

## English translation

I came, I saw, I conquered.

## Author and attribution

Julius Caesar, as reported by ancient biographers

## Meaning

Three short verbs present a victory as swift and decisive. The matching sounds make the announcement memorable and emphatic.

## Cultural context

Caesar defeated Pharnaces II of Pontus at Zela in 47 BCE. Plutarch associates the words with a report to a friend; Suetonius records them on a placard in Caesar’s Pontic triumph.

## Significance and legacy

The phrase helped frame Caesar as an exceptionally effective commander. It remains a compact formula for effortless-looking success, even when the real work was complex.

## A note on the wording

The accounts describe different occasions for the words; they should not be collapsed into a surviving letter to the Senate.
