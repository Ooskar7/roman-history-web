# Oderint dum metuant

## English translation

Let them hate, so long as they fear.

## Author and attribution

Lucius Accius; later associated with Caligula

## Meaning

A ruler accepts hatred as long as fear secures obedience. It expresses the logic of intimidation rather than rule through trust or consent.

## Cultural context

The line belongs to the tragic tradition of Accius’s Atreus. Suetonius reports that the emperor Caligula repeatedly used the tragic saying.

## Significance and legacy

The words became a vivid emblem of tyranny. Their movement from the stage into imperial biography illustrates how ancient writers used theatrical models to characterize abusive power.

## A note on the wording

Caligula is the famous reported user, not the original author. Suetonius’s hostile biographical portrait is part of the evidence’s context.
