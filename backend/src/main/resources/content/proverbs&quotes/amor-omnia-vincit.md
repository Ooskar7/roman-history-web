# Amor Omnia Vincit

## English translation

Love conquers all.

## Author and attribution

Virgil, in a different word order

## Meaning

Love is imagined as a power stronger than resistance. In its original setting, this includes the suffering of desire rather than a guarantee of romantic happiness.

## Cultural context

Eclogue 10 portrays the poet Gallus overwhelmed by love for Lycoris. Pastoral landscapes and imagined escape do not free him from his passion.

## Significance and legacy

Later readers turned the line into a celebration of love’s supremacy. It remains useful to distinguish that optimistic reading from the original scene of surrender to painful emotion.

## A note on the wording

Virgil writes “omnia vincit Amor” at line 69; the requested form rearranges the same words.
